# CC0 game-asset voice and character-vocal packs

Research date: 2026-10-23. Scope: OpenGameArt, itch.io, Kenney, game-jam packs. Only packs whose license was read on the page are listed as CC0. Nothing was downloaded. Speaker counts, durations and per-clip lengths were not stated on any page unless noted; they are marked "not stated" rather than estimated.

- Kenney Voiceover Pack: Fighter (40+ taunts): https://opengameart.org/content/voiceover-pack-fighter-40-taunts (CC0, 36 phrases, 1 professional speaker per a comment, OGG). Already used.

## Candidates with license conflicts or unclear terms (hold, do not use without clarification)

- Warrior Voice Pack (johncarroll): https://johncarroll.itch.io/warrior-voice-pack
  - License field says CC0 ("Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal"), but the description says "reselling or incorporating is prohibited." CC0 cannot carry that restriction, so the terms conflict. Over 110 WAV clips. Speakers and durations not stated. Name-your-own-price, ZIP 18 MB.
- Orc Voice Pack (johncarroll): https://johncarroll.itch.io/orc-voice-pack
  - Same pattern: CC0 field plus "reselling or incorporating is prohibited." Over 90 WAV clips.
- Ghost Voice Pack (Sorth): https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-voice-pack
  - Lists CC0 and says "Do whatever you want." But a commenter (MedicineStorm, 2024-11-14) says the licensing for the voice work is unclear and the pack must be removed unless the origin is indicated. The author replied "Yes, this is my voice," and the commenter responded "Nope!" Provenance is disputed. Reject unless the provenance is resolved. ZIP 5.3 MB, clip count not stated.
- Dying Voices - Female - RPG (Galacti-Chron, voiced by Sky Rae): https://opengameart.org/content/dying-voices-female-rpg
  - Notice says "licensed under cc0 (Public Domain)" and commercial use is allowed, but the page also shows a "CC-BY 3.0" badge and does not say which files carry which license. 54 female death voice clips, single professional speaker (Sky Rae). Format probably MP3 (preview filenames). ZIP 2.2 MB. Ask the author which files are CC0 before use.
- Sci-Fi RTS War Unit Sounds (qubodup): https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-rts-war-unit-sounds
  - CC0 since 2024-08-28 (changed from CC-BY-SA 3.0/GPL2+). Mostly unit and environment SFX, not vocals. The *.template.wav files are radio-voice bases that the author says do not contain his voice. Speaker count not stated. 44.1 kHz WAV, 2.4 MB 7z. Not a voice pack; low value.
