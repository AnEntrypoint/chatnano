# CC0 / Public-Domain Acted Character Speech Sources

Scope: CC0 or public-domain acted or character-style speech usable as voice-reference clips (deep, gravelly, elderly, child, young female, brute/dwarf, villain, creature, robot, ethereal). Not audiobook narration. Licenses were checked on the source page where fetched. Items marked "unverified" were seen only in search snippets.

## Verified CC0 candidates (license read on the page)

- **Steampunk Fantasy Voices** (xathien), OpenGameArt. License: "CC0". 87 individual WAV files plus a 16.9 MB ZIP. Tags: goblin, monster, hero; files named Hero_, Knight_, Archer_, Minion_, Dragon_, Paladin_. Direct ZIP: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Steampunk%20Fantasy%20Voices.zip . Page: https://opengameart.org/content/steampunk-fantasy-voices . Best fit so far for brute, minion, and creature-leaning delivery. Per-file character type is not mapped on the page; listen before use.
- **Ghost Moans** (qubodup), OpenGameArt. License: "CC0". Single ZIP of about 4 MB. Described as "from general ghost banter to dismissive frisky comments by ghosts". Audio format not stated. Direct: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/qubodup-GhostMoans.zip . Page: https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-monster-voice-moaning-growling . Fit: ethereal and spirit.
- **Sci-Fi Voice Lines** (lentikula), itch.io. Author's text: "I release this pack under a CC0 license, so you can use it in whatever project without attribution". 50 WAV files, 48 kHz / 24-bit: 25 robotic female lines and 25 robotic male lines, human-recorded (no generative AI, per author). Download button goes to a "Name your own price" purchase page; a free no-login route was not confirmed. Page: https://lentikula.itch.io/sci-fi-ship-voices . Fit: robot.
- **Warrior Voice Pack** (johncarroll), itch.io. License: "Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal". Over 110 WAV recordings in one 18 MB ZIP. Character: "a warrior dude" with attacks, defense, hits, deaths, a range of emotions. Speaker count not stated. Download is "Name your own price" with a purchase-page button; a free no-login route was not confirmed. Page: https://johncarroll.itch.io/warrior-voice-pack . Fit: fighter or brute (we already have an acted fighter, so lower priority).

## Public domain (recording rights need care)

- **Lux Radio Theater, 1936 episodes** (archive.org item Lux01). Usage field: "Public Domain". 31 MP3 episodes (24 and 32 kbps options) plus 30 OGG Vorbis; 107 files total. Per-file MP3 download needs no login. Date range 1935 to 1936 (uploader's note). Full-cast dramatic acting, adult-leaning, mostly speaking-style dialogue with varied character voices. Page: https://archive.org/details/Lux01 . Caveat: "Public Domain" is the uploader's claim. Old radio recordings can carry separate rights, so check each episode. Strip the surrounding ads and announcer segments before use.
- **Winnie-the-Pooh (1926) dramatic reading** on Wikimedia Commons (chapters 04 and 07) and on archive.org (https://archive.org/details/winnie-the-pooh_dr_2306_librivox). Full-cast LibriVox readings with one character per reader. The text is US public domain (pre-1929). The Commons page says that tag should not be used for sound recordings, so the recording's status is unclear. Verify the LibriVox license on the item page before use. Fit: child and elderly character voices, but these are amateur reads, so quality varies.
