# Five CC0 packs fill the gaps, but deep male and elderly voices stay open

The best CC0 set we can assemble today is small: five packs with a license read on the page, covering two bright and mature female voices, two robotic voice pairs, a creature and brute set, and a ghost set. Two more CC0 items (CoVoST and Common Voice) are speech corpora, not character packs, and Common Voice's download route is unverified. Several packs carry a CC0 label that conflicts with other terms on the same page, so they are held, not used. We found no verified CC0 very-deep male, elderly, or child acted speech. Those roles need either a new search or a clarified license before we can claim a CC0 reference for them. Speaker counts and clip durations were not verified for most packs, so the "distinct character" claim still needs a listening pass and local measurement.

## Recommended set: five verified CC0 packs

The following packs have a CC0 license field or author statement read on the page. None of them is a voice pack we already use. The Kenney, OpenGameArt Voiceover, and cicifyre RPG Starter packs are excluded because they are already in use.

The two cicifyre packs give us two distinct female timbres, and the Steampunk set gives brute and minion delivery that no other CC0 pack covers. Ghost Moans covers the ethereal role. The lentikula pair is useful as a robot contrast, but its robotic delivery makes it a weak human-voice reference. Speaker counts for the cicifyre packs are inferred from the titles only, and no clip length was stated for any of the five.

## Held: CC0 labels that conflict with other terms, or unclear provenance

These packs should not be used until the author clarifies. Each is listed with the conflicting evidence.

The Warrior Voice Pack and Orc Voice Pack (johncarroll) carry a CC0 field, but their descriptions say "reselling or incorporating is prohibited." CC0 cannot carry that restriction, so the two terms conflict ([Warrior](https://johncarroll.itch.io/warrior-voice-pack), [Orc](https://johncarroll.itch.io/orc-voice-pack)). The Warrior pack has over 110 WAV clips, and the Orc pack has over 90. We already have an acted fighter, so the Warrior pack adds little even if it clears.

Dying Voices - Female - RPG (Galacti-Chron, voiced by Sky Rae) lists "licensed under cc0 (Public Domain)" in the notice but also shows a "CC-BY 3.0" badge, and the page does not say which files carry which license ([page](https://opengameart.org/content/dying-voices-female-rpg)). It has 54 female death clips from one professional speaker. Undead trader quotes (Brandon Morris) list both "OGA-BY 3.0" and "CC0" on the page, so they are ambiguous too; the page URL was not recorded in the notes.

The Ghost Voice Pack (Sorth) says "Do whatever you want," but a commenter wrote that the voice licensing "is unclear" and the author's claim of ownership was disputed ([page](https://opengameart.org/content/ghost-voice-pack)). Provenance is unresolved, so this one stays out. Background Voices (pauliuw) is CC0 but contains only 3 clips, which is too small to serve as a set.

## Rejected: non-CC0 terms, no license, or wrong medium

Several packs fail the license test on the page. Jessica's Basic Voice Clips is "cc-by." Voices Sound Effects Library is CC-BY 3.0. Deep System Computer Voice is CC-BY 3.0. The devil/monster line (Gourab Sutradhar) is CC-BY 3.0 per a search snippet, not a fetched page. The Generic Male Voice Pack says "DO NOT redistribute or resale," and the Happy Girl pack requires credit and bars resale. The free generic female pack (r0o0kie) has no license field, and WitPOP is non-commercial only. Military Voice Pack 2 keeps its license inside a PDF in a 47 MB download, so we could not read it and it is on hold, not rejected. The Sci-Fi RTS War Unit Sounds pack is CC0 but is mostly unit and environment SFX, not voice.

The speech corpora also fail. Hi-Fi TTS and LibriTTS are CC BY 4.0. Speech Wikimedia is CC BY-SA. Spoken Wikipedia on Commons is CC BY-SA. Tatoeba audio carries each contributor's chosen license, which includes CC BY-NC. VoxForge releases audio under the GPL. Thorsten-Voice is CC0 but has a single speaker, so it fails the many-speakers goal.

## CoVoST and Common Voice: CC0 metadata, login unverified

CoVoST is CC0 for its annotations: "We've released the data under CC0 license" ([Meta AI](https://ai.meta.com/tools/covost/)). Its audio, however, comes from Common Voice and is not new, so CoVoST adds translation labels but no new voices ([CoVoST GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/covost)). Common Voice is listed as CC0 by Wikipedia ([Common Voice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Voice)). Its many-speaker coverage is the strongest case for distinctness among sentence-level sources, but per-speaker clip length is unverified and typically short. The official download route was not fetched successfully, so its login status is unverified. You asked us to note that Freesound and Common Voice downloads need a login or key. We did not verify that for Freesound, and the Common Voice gap above means we cannot confirm it either.

## Gaps: the roles we still cannot cover under CC0

No verified CC0 very-deep male, gravelly, or elderly acted voice came up in this pass. No verified child voice did either. Public-domain options exist but carry rights questions. The 1935 to 1936 Lux Radio Theater episodes on archive.org are marked "Public Domain," but that is the uploader's claim, and old radio recordings can carry separate rights. The episodes are full-cast, adult-leaning dramatic acting, and their surrounding ads and announcer segments would need stripping. The LibriVox Pooh dramatic reading fits child and elderly roles, but its recording status is unclear, and the Commons page warns the tag should not be used for sound recordings. The "Good CC0 Voice" collection is a curator list and states that attribution is the user's responsibility, so its linked packs are unverified.

## Next steps

Listen to and measure the five verified packs in a scratch folder: confirm speaker counts, durations, and file format with ffprobe, and confirm that each character is distinct. Ask johncarroll whether the "reselling or incorporating" clause is withdrawn, and ask the Dying Voices author which files are CC0. The unverified itch.io voice listings from the tag page should be opened next, starting with the packs labeled as character or vocal sets. For the deep male and elderly roles, search OpenGameArt and itch.io with the CC0 license filter and check per-file licenses before downloading.
